Label definitions
1 ASSERTED
The answer presents the figure as fact, or relies on it for a conclusion or recommendation, even with a vague caveat elsewhere.
2 REFUTED
The answer names the figure in order to reject, correct or warn against it.
3 NEUTRAL
The answer attributes the figure to its source and gives no verdict either way.
4 UNRELATED
The matching number is not this claim at all, e.g. a correctly derived figure that shares the digits.

You are not judging whether the claim is true, only what the answer does with it.
