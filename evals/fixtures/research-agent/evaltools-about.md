# About EvalTools

EvalTools is a hosted evaluation platform for teams shipping LLM agents to
production. Founded in 2024, based in Berlin, 31 employees.

We started because our own agents kept passing demos and failing customers.
The gap was never the model — it was that nobody could say, with a number,
whether last week's prompt change made things better or worse.

## What we do

- Golden datasets with versioning and diff
- Outcome and trajectory scoring out of the box
- LLM-as-judge with human-label calibration
- CI gates that block a merge on regression

## What we do not do

We are not an observability vendor and we do not host models. We integrate
with OpenTelemetry rather than replacing it.

Customers include mid-market SaaS teams across the EU and US. We publish our
own agent pass rates quarterly, including the quarters that went backwards.
