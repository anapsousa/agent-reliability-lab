# Agent Evaluation Tooling: Market Sizing 2026–2030

*Northgate Analytics — subscriber note, 15 July 2026*

## Abstract

The agent evaluation and observability tooling market reached an estimated
$310M in 2025. We model a 2030 range of $2.4bn–$4.1bn, with the spread driven
almost entirely by whether evaluation consolidates into existing observability
suites or persists as a standalone category.

Full analysis, segment breakdown and vendor share estimates are available to
subscribers.
