# Most teams still ship agents blind, survey finds

*TechWire — 2 June 2026*

A survey of 890 engineering organisations finds that **48% run no automated
evaluation of their LLM agents**, down from the 62% reported by AIQ Report in
March.

The gap between the two figures is partly methodological. TechWire's sample
was drawn from companies with more than 200 employees, while AIQ surveyed
teams of all sizes through practitioner newsletters. Smaller teams are
markedly less likely to have an evaluation pipeline.

Both reports agree on the direction: adoption is rising, and trajectory-level
scoring remains rare. TechWire puts teams scoring trajectories at 13%,
against AIQ's 9%.

"The number everyone quotes depends entirely on who you asked," said one
analyst who declined to be named.
